DEMO DOCUMENT. A fictional library, written for the CiteCheck demo.

Opening hours

The library opens at 9am on weekdays.
The library closes at 6pm on Saturdays.

Borrowing

Members can borrow up to 8 books at a time.
Loans last 21 days.
Overdue books are not charged a fine.

Room bookings (2019)

Study rooms cost £5 per hour.
